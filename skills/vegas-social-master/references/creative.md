# Original creative direction

Give each concept a specific audience, visible first-second event, one question, escalation and payoff. Concepts A/B must differ in story or hook, not just wording. Never promise virality.

Before multiple scenes, write a continuity sheet: face/body, exact clothing/product features, room landmarks, light direction, props, time, language and aspect. Repeat relevant locks in every standalone prompt. Different chats/accounts do not share asset memory.

Image prompt: shot scale/viewpoint → subject and exact design → one action/pose → environment geometry → realistic light → necessary fidelity constraints. Use one separate native image per scene. Add exact text/logo in editing when needed.

Video prompt: approved first-frame identity → one camera move or locked camera → one principal action → light/weather continuity → speaker and concise dialogue → relevant environmental sound. Avoid redescribing a source into a different person/product or putting many unrelated cuts into one clip.

Recipes:
- Product: visible-detail hook → material/use proof → hero payoff. Lock shape, count, labels and patterns; restrained macro movement preserves fidelity better than complicated orbits.
- UGC: phone perspective, ordinary room light, restrained handheld movement, a concrete demonstration, conversational line. No invented testimonials/results.
- Fashion: detail → silhouette → movement → full look. Source garment patterns/accessories remain authoritative.
- Wildlife fiction: geography/conflict → escalating action → reversal. Match markings/terrain; avoid impossible anatomy/motion.
- Narrative: establish geography, vary shot scale, preserve screen direction. Dialogue must fit the actual duration.

Inspect outputs for anatomy, product geometry/count, text, light, camera direction, audio clipping, lip sync, pacing, ending and aspect. No prompt guarantees consistency. Repair failed scenes only after reviewing cost.
